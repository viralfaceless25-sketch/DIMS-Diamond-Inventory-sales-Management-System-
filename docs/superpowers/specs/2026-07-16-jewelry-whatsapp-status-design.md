# Jewelry WhatsApp Status Video Design

## Goal

Create a polished WhatsApp Status video that presents the supplied necklaces and pendants in an elegant luxury style.

## Format

- Final delivery: MP4 using H.264 video and AAC audio
- Canvas: 1080 x 1920 portrait
- Target length: 25 to 30 seconds
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- No text, logo, pricing, or branding

## Editorial Direction

- Lead with a strong moving jewelry reveal from the supplied MOV footage.
- Use the best moments from all four video clips, avoiding shaky starts, camera settling, and redundant angles.
- Use only the strongest supporting photos where they add product variety.
- Give still photos restrained slow push-ins or pans so the montage remains alive.
- Use soft dissolves and clean cuts; avoid flashy templates or distracting effects.
- Keep pendants centered and preserve their real appearance.

## Visual Treatment

- Maintain the neutral gray display background and natural gold/diamond color.
- Apply light contrast, warmth, and highlight control without changing the jewelry design.
- Fill the portrait canvas cleanly, avoiding visible bars.
- Start and end with short fades to black.

## Audio

- Use an original, understated luxury-style instrumental bed.
- Keep the music smooth and low-key, without vocals or abrupt beats.
- Fade audio in and out with the picture.
- Do not use the original camera audio.

## Quality Checks

- Verify duration, dimensions, codecs, and frame rate.
- Inspect representative frames for correct orientation, cropping, sharpness, and jewelry placement.
- Confirm audio is present and does not clip.
- Ensure the MP4 plays from beginning to end and is suitable for WhatsApp Status upload.
